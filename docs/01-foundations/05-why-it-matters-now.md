# 5. Why Inference Matters Now

## Generative AI Has Made Inference a Major Engineering Concern

Models are being used:

- In **more products**
- By **more users**
- For **complex tasks**

---

## Three Focuses That Are Particularly Important

### A) AI Moving into Everyday Products

- Search
- Document parsing
- Coding assistants
- Business workflows
- Customer support

→ Increasing use of model inference.

### B) Serving Costs Matter at Scale

- Once a model is used continuously, even small efficiency improvements can produce **substantial savings**.

### C) The Workloads Are Becoming More Complex

- Larger contexts
- Multimodal inputs
- Tool-using agents
- Multiple model calls

→ These can create more demanding inference workloads.

---

## Looking Ahead

In AI applications, **inference engineering practices remain important** as AI expands and scales up.

Engineers continue to work on:

- Faster serving
- Smaller (or) more efficient models
- Specialized hardware
- Distributed systems
- Smarter ways to allocate compute

---

## Key Definitions

| Term                         | Meaning                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI model**                 | The learned mathematical system that produces predictions (or) generates outputs |
| **Inference**                | Using the model to process an input & produce an output                          |
| **Inference infrastructure** | Hardware + software that makes the model available to applications & users       |
| **Inference engineer**       | Making infrastructure fast, efficient, reliable, scalable & cost-efficient       |
